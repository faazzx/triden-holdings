Our History

Where We Come From

Triden Holdings was founded on a simple conviction: that a company built on genuine capability, honest dealing, and relentless execution would always find its place in the market. Starting as a contracting firm in the Gulf region, we earned our reputation project by project — delivering on time, within budget, and to the standard our clients expected.

As our client relationships deepened, so did the scope of what they needed from us. We expanded into trading and services to meet demand for reliable supply chains, then into import and export as our partners sought a trusted hand in navigating global commerce. Today, Triden Holdings operates three integrated divisions under unified leadership — each one a market leader in its own right.

We remain privately held, owner-operated, and deeply committed to the long-term relationships that built this company. Our growth has never come at the expense of our values.
